Refund Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Applies to all website templates sold by Leo Studio on Framer Marketplace and other template marketplaces.

In short: because templates are digital products that are delivered instantly and can be copied, all sales are final. We issue refunds only when a template has a major functional defect that we cannot fix.

1. Digital products are non-refundable

Once a template has been delivered — duplicated, remixed or downloaded into your account — it cannot be returned. For this reason we do not offer refunds for:

  • A change of mind, or buying the wrong template
  • Design taste, colours, fonts or layout preferences
  • Not having the skills or time to customise the template
  • Features that are not described in the listing
  • Issues caused by your own edits, third-party plugins, embeds or services you connect
  • Your platform plan, domain, hosting or billing with the template platform

2. When we do refund

We will issue a full refund if the template has a major functional defect, for example:

  • The template cannot be opened, duplicated or remixed into your account
  • Core features described in the listing do not work (pages, CMS collections, forms or navigation are broken)
  • Pages or sections shown in the listing are missing

We will first try to fix the problem. If we cannot fix it within 7 days of confirming it, you will receive a full refund.

3. How to request help or a refund

Email leostudio0218@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase with:

  • The template name and your order or receipt number
  • A description of the problem and, if possible, screenshots or a link to your project

We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued through the marketplace or payment provider you purchased from, to the original payment method.

4. Your legal rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you.

5. Support

Questions about setup or customisation are welcome at leostudio0218@gmail.com. Each template also includes a setup guide.
